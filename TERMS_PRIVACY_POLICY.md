## 1. About this service

SylviaNG Community is the internal community engagement platform of `[COMPANY]`. It lets
employees view the employee directory, post to the feed, react and comment, give recognitions
and badges, take surveys, vote in elections, manage team tasks, use the marketplace, and
exchange messages. It is provided to employees and authorized personnel only.

## 2. Terms of Use

1. **Eligibility.** Access is limited to current employees and others the organization

2. **Your responsibilities.** Keep your password confidential, use accurate information in

3. **Roles.** What you can see and do depends on your role (Employee, Supervisor, HR, Admin).
   Attempting to bypass role restrictions is prohibited.
4. **Your content.** You remain responsible for what you post, comment, send or upload.
   You grant `[COMPANY]` the right to store and display it within the platform for business
   purposes.
5. **Availability.** The service is provided "as is" for internal use; features may change,
   and downtime may occur for maintenance.
6. **Suspension.** Access may be suspended or removed if you breach this document or company
   policy, or when your employment ends.
7. **Governing law.** `[JURISDICTION / GOVERNING LAW]`.

## 3. Acceptable Use Policy

You must not post or send content that is harassing, discriminatory, threatening, sexually
explicit, defamatory, or that discloses confidential company or personal information without
authorization. You must not impersonate others, upload malware, scrape or bulk-export other
people's data, run spam or unapproved advertising, or list prohibited items in the Marketplace.
Content can be reported (posts, chat messages, marketplace listings) and is reviewed by
authorized moderators (HR/Admin). Violations may lead to content removal, account action,
and disciplinary measures under company policy.
